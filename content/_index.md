# Human-Robot Interaction @ University of Nottingham

HRI@UoN is a community of researchers with interests in Human-Robot Interaction, sited at the School of Computer Science, University of Nottingham, UK. Collectively, our research spans the foundations of robotics, including manipulation, navigation, robot safety, machine learning, and shared autonomy, while driving interdisciplinary explorations in intelligent manufacturing, healthcare, social sciences, and the arts and humanities. We adopt human-centred approaches, investigating the potentials and challenges of bringing robots to human environments, closer to the places people live, work, learn and create. HRI@UoN is comprised of members from several established research groups and initiatives, including the Cyber-physical Health and Assistive Robotics Technologies (CHART), Mixed Reality Lab, and the Somabotics programme.
